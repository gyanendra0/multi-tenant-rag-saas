# GreenLeaf Tea — Product FAQ (excerpt)

## Storage
Store tea in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Properly
stored, our loose-leaf green tea stays fresh for up to 12 months from the date of
opening.

## Brewing
For the best flavor, heat water to 80°C (176°F) — not boiling. Use one teaspoon
of leaves per cup and steep for 2 to 3 minutes. Steeping longer than 3 minutes
can make the tea bitter. The leaves can be re-steeped up to two additional times.

## Caffeine
A single cup of GreenLeaf green tea contains approximately 30 mg of caffeine,
about one third of the caffeine in a typical cup of coffee.
