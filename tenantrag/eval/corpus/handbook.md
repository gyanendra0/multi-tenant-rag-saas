# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

## Vacation and Leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days
begin accruing on the first day of employment and may be carried over up to a
maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Requests must be submitted
at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Employees are also entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. Unused sick days do
not carry over.

## Working Hours
The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Core collaboration
hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Employees may work remotely up to
three days per week with manager approval.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Receipts
are required for any expense over $25. Reimbursements are processed on the 15th
of each month.
